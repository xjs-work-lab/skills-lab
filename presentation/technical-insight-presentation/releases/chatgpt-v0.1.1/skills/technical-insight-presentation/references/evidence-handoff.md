# Evidence handoff / PPT claims
For each decision-relevant claim record `claim_id`, exact wording, FACT/CLAIM/OBSERVATION/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS, primary source name and URL, Fig/Table/section, measured system/workload/model/device, exact baseline and metric unit/level, proof limit, strongest competing explanation, confidence, technical actor and presentation form. Accept only approved decision summaries and source-linked details; no untracked facts from decorative diagrams. Academic Insight remains the research authority; this presentation workflow is the representation layer.
