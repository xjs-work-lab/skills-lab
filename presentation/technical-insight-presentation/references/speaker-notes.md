# Speaker notes are the technical teaching layer (mandatory)

Actual PowerPoint Notes on each slide must follow this order:

## 一、页面内容详细讲解
Explain this particular screen in visual read order. For every meaningful card/arrow/chart/branch/figure, explain source/target/actor/action/condition/output, why it matters, the mechanism, user/system metric, competing alternative, limitations, and connection to the next page. Every numerical comparison needs platform/workload/device/model/software/backend, units, comparator, aggregation level, experiment vs conceptual-example qualification. Notes MUST say which interpretation is our research synthesis and which is directly in an original source. Not just paraphrase the headline or paste abstracts.

## 二、来源逐条解读
One record **per genuinely relevant original source**: type, bibliographic title, authors/institution, venue/year/version, original URL, exact Fig/Table/section, source question and experimental method, baseline, quantitative support, precise location on THIS slide, source cannot establish, corroboration independence, bounded analyst synthesis. Official SDK documentation is not a research paper; patent disclosure ≠ deployed product; same-group papers may not be independent replicates.

Cover/agenda notes explain goal, scope, chapter logic and numbering; no fake sources. Optional technical Q&A can come afterward. Notes must be embedded in actual PPTX and text-export verified separately. A long note is not automatically a good note; human-check correspondence with slide objects.
