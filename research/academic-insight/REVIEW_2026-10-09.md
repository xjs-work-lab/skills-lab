**No GitHub migration/structure-rebuild mechanics belong in Academic Insight.** Research information traceability is part of the method; moving repositories, branch cutover, or file migrations are project operations, not insight methodology.

## What v0.7.1 already does well — keep, do not rewrite

1. **Field map first**, authoritative journals/conferences/scholars/groups before broad query expansion; prestige guides discovery but is not proof.
2. Five-Look-Three-Set, goal lock and seven anti-drift checks; research must produce decisions, not simply paper abstracts.
3. Source/claim/capability/direction distinction, five-level evidence grammar, direct paper/patent/vendor original links.
4. Paper/Patent 10Q cards, direct prior-art claims, lineage, negative evidence and comparable metrics.
5. Keep/Upgrade/Downgrade/Narrow/Kill, evidence maturity, confidence separate from capability maturity.
6. Object/relation-style provenance and current-state versus historical-decision distinction.
7. Cross-source synthesis and the strongest competing hypotheses, rather than equating popularity or novelty with importance.

## Gaps exposed by the smartphone CPU/uArch case

| Priority | Existing coverage | Gap / failure mode observed | Suggested addition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | §17 assumes smallest discriminating experiment | The user explicitly forbade any owned experiment, simulation, phone measurement or PoC; a mandatory experimental next-step would make decision closure impossible | Define **Evidence Mode** at Goal Lock: `PUBLIC_ONLY`, `EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_ONLY`, or `EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED`; never infer authorization to run tests |
| **P0** | §§12–18 discuss competing software and architecture | A performance bottleneck is too easily promoted to a new hardware design, even though optimized compiler/runtime/OS/kernel alternatives exist | Introduce a **four-gate physical-hardware necessity test**: target occurrence → strongest alternatives exhausted → irreducible physical control/state residual → full-system gain vs costs |
| **P0** | §28 expects primary bets | Target number can quietly become a quota, inflating ordinary enablement into novel hardware | **Zero is a valid output**. Separate actionable engineering tracks, conditional reserves, watch, and unsupported hardware claims |
| **P0** | §4 fact/claim/inference/hypothesis | First-party API existence, vendor messaging, research mock-up and measured full-system behavior can be blended under “evidence” | Add claim-specific **proof scope ledger**: source type, EXACT supported proposition, metric layer, system boundary, uncertainty, excluded inference |
| **P1** | §§13/14 metric normalization | Single kernel, aggregate operators, stage, end-to-end application and phone-day battery/QoE often need different narrative framing | Mandatory **measurement scope/type vector** with device/model/backend/precision/baseline/aggregation/units; do not sum incomparable gains |
| **P1** | §26 confidence audit includes corroboration | Several related papers may share a group/software or device; counting citations overstates independent corroboration | Track **evidence independence clusters** (author, artifact, code, measurement campaign, vendor) and distinct vendor documents vs peer-reviewed results |
| **P1** | §27 stage review is broad | Progress could be reported as many files rather than answered questions; gap may remain inherently unresolvable with public sources | Define finite **decision-question closure** with `CLOSED_WITH_BOUNDARY`, not only `COMPLETE` or “need experiment” |
| **P1** | §28 strategy | Need to map conclusions to controllable technical ownership without inventing internal management approvals or budget commitments | Add **owner-domain/decision-authority split**: recommendation in CPU/LLVM vs Runtime/OS vs App/Agent, separate from organization assignment |
| **P2** | final deliverable is technical report | Handoff to a leadership deck can drift from source claims or hide caveats in tiny citations | Optional **evidence-backed presentation handoff package**, consumed by a separate Skill; do not embed slide generation in Academic Insight |

## Case-specific transferable lessons

- Three priority engineering/platform capabilities can coexist with **zero public-evidence-qualified new ISA/silicon proposals**.
- A strong NPU/OS or LLVM/SME baseline does **not** destroy CPU-related engineering value; it narrows which *new physical mechanism* remains unexplained.
- “An API exists” does not imply “the user target was fulfilled” or “the complete workload was evaluated”.
- Formal decision closure can be valid even when novelty-specific hardware evidence remains a `GAP`: close the management question **with a public-evidence confidence ceiling**, not invent a new test.
- A final leadership slide and its detailed speaker notes are separate communication artifacts; their production should not change underlying research status.
