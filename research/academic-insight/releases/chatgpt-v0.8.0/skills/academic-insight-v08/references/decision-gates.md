# v0.8 evidence & closure checks

For each important statement record:
- source identity: exact title, author/group, publication type/status/year, original URL, Fig/Table/section;
- claim class FACT/CLAIM/OBSERVATION/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS;
- direct proof scope and what is *NOT* supported;
- measured layer, workload/device/model/backend/precision/units/statistic/comparator and method overhead;
- author/artifact/vendor independence cluster and counterevidence;
- strongest existing technical alternative, remaining residual and causal test when relevant;
- (hardware-only) physical necessity gates 1–4, with missing flags, and silicon benefit/cost boundary;
- decision question, actionable engineer/control layer, evidence confidence vs capability maturity;
- closure CLOSED/CLOSED_WITH_BOUNDARY/PARTIAL/OPEN, Kill/Keep/Narrow and evidence reopening trigger;
- authorized mode: PUBLIC_ONLY, EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_ONLY, EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED.

**Public-only example:** Published research shows a specific kernel is faster. This FACT does not establish an entire phone Agent task QoE win. A strong compiler/NPU/runtime route may be actionable while a special hardware register remains unsupported. Conclude engineering priority + conditional hardware hypothesis, not fabricated measurements.
